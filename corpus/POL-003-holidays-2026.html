Company Holiday Calendar 2026

Lumora Systems, Inc. is a fictional company. This document is synthetic and was written for an AI engineering course project.

1. Overview

Lumora observes paid company holidays based on each employee's registered work location (see Employment Classifications, POL-001, Section 7). Company holidays are paid days off and do not reduce an employee's PTO balance. Holidays do not need to be requested in PeopleHub. If a holiday falls during approved PTO, the holiday is not deducted from the PTO balance (see PTO Policy, POL-002, Section 8).

Full-time and part-time employees receive holiday pay. Part-time employees receive holiday pay for holidays that fall on a regularly scheduled workday. Interns receive holiday pay for holidays that fall within their program dates. Contractors are not paid for company holidays unless their contract states otherwise.

5. Floating Holidays

In addition to the fixed holidays above, full-time employees in all countries receive 2 floating holidays per calendar year. Part-time employees with an FTE of 0.75 or higher receive 1 floating holiday. Interns and contractors do not receive floating holidays.

  • Floating holidays may be used for cultural or religious observances, personal milestones, or any other reason.
  • Floating holidays must be requested in PeopleHub with at least 5 business days of notice and require manager approval.
  • Floating holidays do not carry over. Unused floating holidays are forfeited on December 31 and are not paid out at separation.
  • Employees hired after September 30 receive 1 floating holiday for that calendar year.

6. Working on a Holiday

Employees who are required to work on a company holiday (for example, as part of an on-call rotation) receive a substitute day off, which must be taken within 60 days. Non-exempt US employees who work on a holiday are also paid at 1.5 times their regular rate for hours worked. Canadian employees receive statutory holiday premium pay as required by provincial employment standards.

7. Holidays While Traveling or Working Abroad

Employees who are temporarily working from another state or country under an approved arrangement (Remote Work Policy, POL-004; Work From Abroad and Cross-Border Policy, POL-005) observe the holiday calendar of their registered work location, not the local holidays of the place where they are temporarily working.

8. Year-End Recharge Week

Lumora does not schedule a company-wide closure in 2026. Teams are encouraged to minimize meetings between December 28 and December 31, 2026, so that employees who choose to take PTO during that period can do so with less disruption. PTO taken during that period follows the standard PTO Policy (POL-002) and must be requested in advance.
