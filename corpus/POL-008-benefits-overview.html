Benefits Overview 2026

Lumora Systems, Inc. is a fictional company. This document is synthetic and was written for an AI engineering course project. Plan names and amounts are invented.

1. About This Overview

This overview summarizes the benefits Lumora offers to employees in the United States for the 2026 plan year (January 1 to December 31, 2026). It is a summary only. If there is any difference between this overview and the official plan documents, the plan documents govern. Employees in Canada and the United Kingdom receive country-specific benefits described in local supplements; Section 9 gives a short summary.

2. Eligibility

Eligibility depends on employment classification (see Employment Classifications, POL-001):

  • Full-time employees are eligible for all benefits in this overview.
  • Part-time employees with an FTE of 0.75 or higher (30 or more scheduled hours per week) are eligible for medical, dental, vision, life insurance, the 401(k) plan, and the Employee Assistance Program. They are not eligible for tuition reimbursement.
  • Part-time employees with an FTE below 0.75 are eligible for the 401(k) plan and the Employee Assistance Program only.
  • Interns are eligible for the Employee Assistance Program only.
  • Contractors are not eligible for Lumora benefits but may use the Employee Assistance Program while actively engaged.

Eligible dependents include a legal spouse or registered domestic partner, and children up to age 26.

3. When Coverage Starts and Ends

Medical, dental, and vision coverage begins on the first day of the month following the employee's start date. An employee who starts on the first day of a month is covered from that day. New employees must enroll within 30 days of their start date; employees who do not enroll are automatically enrolled in employee-only coverage under the Basic PPO plan, with no dental or vision coverage.

Coverage ends on the last day of the month in which employment ends or the employee becomes ineligible. Continuation coverage (COBRA) information is mailed within 14 days of the loss of coverage.

5. Dental and Vision

Dental coverage includes two preventive cleanings per year at no cost, 80% coverage for basic services, and 50% coverage for major services, with a $2,000 annual maximum per person. Orthodontia is covered at 50% for children up to age 19 with a $2,000 lifetime maximum.

Vision coverage includes an annual eye exam with a $10 copay and a $200 annual allowance for frames or contact lenses.

6. Retirement: 401(k) Plan

All US employees except interns are eligible to contribute to the Lumora 401(k) plan from their first paycheck. Lumora matches 100% of the first 4% of eligible pay that the employee contributes, plus 50% of the next 2%, for a maximum Company match of 5% of eligible pay. Company matching contributions vest immediately. New employees are automatically enrolled at a 4% contribution rate unless they opt out within 30 days.

7. Other Benefits

7.1 Life and disability insurance

Basic life insurance of 2 times annual base salary (up to $500,000) is provided at no cost to full-time and benefits-eligible part-time employees. Short-term disability pays 60% of base salary after a 7-day waiting period for up to 12 weeks. Long-term disability pays 60% of base salary after 90 days. See the Leave of Absence Policy (POL-009) for how disability interacts with leave.

7.2 Employee Assistance Program (EAP)

The EAP offers free, confidential counseling (up to 8 sessions per issue per year), legal and financial consultations, and referrals. It is available 24/7 to all workers, including interns and contractors, and to members of their household.

7.3 Wellness stipend

Full-time employees receive a wellness stipend of $50 per month, paid through payroll, for gym memberships, fitness classes, meditation apps, or similar wellness expenses. No receipts are required.

7.4 Tuition reimbursement

Full-time employees with at least 6 months of tenure may receive tuition reimbursement of up to $5,250 per calendar year for accredited degree or certificate programs related to their current role or a future role at Lumora. Courses must be approved in advance by the employee's manager and People Operations, and the employee must earn a grade of B or better (or "pass" in pass/fail courses). Employees who leave Lumora voluntarily within 12 months of receiving reimbursement must repay it. Tuition reimbursement is separate from the $1,500 annual learning budget described in POL-007, Section 6.4.

7.5 Commuter benefits

Employees may set aside pre-tax money for transit and parking up to the IRS monthly limits.

8. Changing Your Elections

Benefit elections are made for the full plan year. Employees may change elections only during:

  • Open enrollment, held from November 2 to November 16, 2026, for the 2027 plan year; or
  • within 30 days of a qualifying life event, such as marriage, divorce, birth or adoption of a child, death of a dependent, loss of other coverage, or a change in employment classification that affects eligibility.

Changes are made in PeopleHub under Benefits → Life Events. Supporting documentation (such as a birth certificate or marriage certificate) must be uploaded within 30 days of the event. Requests made after 30 days will be considered only at the next open enrollment.

9. Canada and United Kingdom (Summary)

  • Canada: Employees receive provincial-plan supplemental health and dental coverage starting on day one, a Group RRSP with a 4% Company match, and the same EAP and wellness stipend.
  • United Kingdom: Employees receive private medical insurance, a workplace pension with a 5% employer contribution, life assurance of 4 times salary, and the same EAP and wellness stipend.

For details, contact People Operations through an HR ticket in the "Benefits" category.

10. Getting Help

For questions about claims, contact the carrier listed on your insurance card. For eligibility, enrollment, or life event questions, open an HR ticket in the "Benefits" category; Total Rewards responds within 3 business days. During open enrollment, Total Rewards hosts daily office hours.
