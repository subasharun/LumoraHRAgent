# Expense Reimbursement, Travel, and Stipends Policy

> Lumora Systems, Inc. is a fictional company. This document is synthetic and was written for an AI engineering course project.

## 1. Purpose

This policy explains which business expenses Lumora reimburses, the limits that apply, how to submit claims, and the stipends available to eligible employees. The guiding principle is simple: spend Company money as carefully as you would spend your own, and only for legitimate business purposes.

## 2. Who This Policy Covers

This policy applies to all employees. Interns may be reimbursed for pre-approved business travel only. Contractors may be reimbursed only where their statement of work explicitly allows it, and then under the limits in this policy. Stipend eligibility is defined in Section 6 and in the Employment Classifications Policy (POL-001).

## 3. General Rules

- Expenses must be **necessary, reasonable, and directly related to Lumora business**.
- Claims must be submitted in the Expensify-style "Lumora Expenses" tool within **30 calendar days** of the expense date. Claims submitted after 90 days will not be reimbursed.
- An itemized receipt is required for every expense of **$25 or more** (or the local currency equivalent). Credit card statements are not receipts.
- Expenses are approved by the employee's manager up to **$5,000 per claim**. Claims above $5,000 also require department director approval.
- Employees may not approve their own expenses, split a purchase to stay under an approval limit, or claim an expense that was reimbursed by a third party.
- Reimbursements are paid in the next payroll cycle after approval.

## 4. Non-Reimbursable Expenses

The following are never reimbursed:

- **personal computers, laptops, tablets, and phones** (Lumora issues these through IT; see POL-011);
- personal entertainment, including in-flight entertainment and hotel movies;
- alcohol, except as part of approved client entertainment (Section 5.5);
- traffic and parking fines, and penalties of any kind;
- seat, class, or room upgrades that are not covered in Section 5;
- personal grooming, clothing, and luggage;
- gifts to colleagues, except through the recognition program;
- travel, lodging, or living costs during personal Work From Abroad periods (POL-005, Section 10);
- expenses of family members or guests.

## 5. Business Travel

### 5.1 Approval and booking

All business travel must be approved in advance by the employee's manager. International travel also requires department director approval. Flights and hotels must be booked through the Lumora Travel Portal so that the Company can locate travelers in an emergency.

### 5.2 Air travel

- Flights of **6 hours or less**: economy class.
- Flights **over 6 hours**: premium economy class is permitted.
- Flights **over 10 hours**: business class is permitted with VP approval.
- Book at least 14 days in advance where possible. Change fees caused by personal preference are not reimbursed.

### 5.3 Lodging

| Location | Nightly cap (before taxes) |
|---|---|
| Standard US, Canada, and UK cities | $250 |
| High-cost cities: New York, San Francisco, Boston, Seattle, London, Zurich, Tokyo | $350 |

Rates above the cap require manager approval before booking, with a written reason (for example, a conference hotel block).

### 5.4 Meals and per diem

Meals during business travel are reimbursed at actual cost up to a **daily limit of $75 for domestic travel and $100 for international travel**. Meals provided by a conference or client do not count toward the limit but may not be claimed. Itemized receipts are required for meals of $25 or more.

### 5.5 Client entertainment

Client meals and entertainment require manager pre-approval, a list of attendees and their companies, and a business purpose. The limit is $150 per person including alcohol. Alcohol may not exceed 30% of the total bill.

### 5.6 Ground transportation and mileage

Taxis, ride-share, and public transit are reimbursed for business travel. Personal vehicle use for business is reimbursed at the IRS standard mileage rate (US), the CRA rate (Canada), or the HMRC rate (UK). Commuting between home and the employee's regular office is not reimbursable.

## 6. Stipends and Allowances

### 6.1 One-time home office stipend

Eligible employees may claim up to **$500** once (not once per year) for home office furniture and accessories.

**Eligibility:** full-time employees and part-time employees with an FTE of 0.75 or higher, who have a **hybrid or remote** work arrangement in PeopleHub (POL-004, Section 2). Office-based employees, part-time employees below 0.75 FTE, interns, and contractors are not eligible.

**Timing:** the claim must be submitted within **12 months** of the employee's start date or the date their hybrid or remote arrangement began, whichever is later.

**Eligible items and sub-limits within the $500 stipend:**

| Item | Maximum |
|---|---|
| Ergonomic office chair | $350 |
| Desk or standing desk | $300 |
| Desk lamp, keyboard, mouse, headset, webcam, cables | $150 combined |
| Footrest, monitor arm, laptop stand | $100 combined |

**Not covered by the stipend:** laptops, tablets, phones, and monitors (IT provides one external monitor to hybrid and remote employees on request; see POL-011), printers, internet installation, decor, and general household furniture. Items purchased with the stipend belong to the employee.

### 6.2 Internet allowance

Employees with a **remote** arrangement (not hybrid) receive an internet allowance of **$50 per month**, paid through payroll. No receipt is required, but the employee confirms annually that they maintain a reliable connection.

### 6.3 Coworking allowance

Employees with a **remote** arrangement may claim coworking space costs up to **$150 per month**, with a receipt. Coworking spaces must offer a private room for confidential calls (POL-004, Section 9).

### 6.4 Learning budget

Full-time employees and part-time employees with an FTE of 0.75 or higher receive a **$1,500 per calendar year** learning budget for courses, books, certifications, and conference fees related to their current role or a role they are developing toward at Lumora. Conference travel funded from the learning budget follows the travel rules in Section 5. Unused learning budget does not carry over. Tuition reimbursement for degree programs is a separate benefit described in the Benefits Overview (POL-008).

## 7. Corporate Cards

Employees who travel more than four times per year may request a Lumora corporate card. Corporate cards are for business expenses only. Any personal charge must be reported within 5 business days and repaid in full.

## 8. Submitting a Claim

1. Open Lumora Expenses and select the correct category (Travel, Meals, Home Office Stipend, Learning, Client Entertainment, Other).
2. Attach itemized receipts for every expense of $25 or more.
3. Add a short business purpose, and for client entertainment, the attendees.
4. Submit within 30 days. Your manager receives the claim automatically.

## 9. Audits and Misuse

Finance audits a sample of claims each month. Submitting false or inflated claims is serious misconduct and may result in termination and repayment.

## 10. Frequently Asked Questions

**Can I expense a new laptop because mine is slow?** No. Laptops are never reimbursed. Request a replacement or upgrade from IT through the IT Service Desk (POL-011, Section 4).

**Can I expense a $400 office chair?** If you are eligible for the home office stipend, up to $350 of the chair cost can be reimbursed from your $500 one-time stipend. The remaining $50 is not reimbursable.

**I am hybrid. Do I get the internet allowance?** No. The internet allowance is only for employees with a remote arrangement. Hybrid employees are eligible for the one-time home office stipend.
