# Leave of Absence Policy

> Lumora Systems, Inc. is a fictional company. This document is synthetic and was written for an AI engineering course project.

## 1. Purpose

This policy describes the types of leave available to Lumora employees other than paid time off (PTO) and company holidays. PTO is covered by the PTO Policy (POL-002) and holidays by the Holiday Calendar (POL-003). Where federal, state, provincial, or national law provides a greater entitlement than this policy, the law applies.

Leave reasons, medical information, and supporting documents are handled as Restricted data (POL-006, Section 3) and are visible only to the Leave team in People Operations. Managers are told the dates of leave, not the reason, unless the employee chooses to share it.

## 2. Sick Leave

### 2.1 Entitlement

| Classification | Sick leave per calendar year |
|---|---|
| Full-time | 10 days |
| Part-time | 10 days multiplied by FTE |
| Intern | 5 days per program |
| Contractor | None (per contract) |

Sick leave is granted in full on January 1 (or on the start date, prorated by month for new hires). Unused sick leave does not carry over and is not paid out at separation, except where local law requires.

### 2.2 Using sick leave

Sick leave may be used for the employee's own illness, injury, medical appointments, or mental health, and to care for an ill family member. Employees should notify their manager as early as possible, ideally before the start of their workday, and record the absence in PeopleHub within 2 business days of returning. No doctor's note is required for absences of 3 consecutive workdays or fewer. Absences of more than 3 consecutive workdays require a note from a healthcare provider, uploaded to PeopleHub (it is visible only to the Leave team).

If sick leave is exhausted, employees may use PTO, and for longer illnesses should contact the Leave team about medical leave (Section 4).

## 3. Parental Leave

### 3.1 Eligibility

Full-time and part-time employees who have completed **6 months** of continuous service by the date of birth, adoption, or foster placement are eligible for paid parental leave. Interns and contractors are not eligible.

### 3.2 Paid leave amounts

- **Birthing parent:** up to **16 weeks** of paid parental leave at 100% of base salary. This runs concurrently with any short-term disability benefit and any statutory leave.
- **Non-birthing parent** (including adoptive and foster parents): up to **12 weeks** of paid parental leave at 100% of base salary.

Part-time employees receive leave pay based on their scheduled hours.

### 3.3 Timing

Parental leave must begin within **12 months** of the birth or placement. It may be taken in one continuous block or in up to two blocks of at least 2 weeks each. Employees should notify their manager and submit a leave request in PeopleHub at least **30 calendar days** before the expected start, or as soon as possible if the timing is unforeseeable.

### 3.4 Returning to work

Employees returning from parental leave may request a phased return of up to 4 weeks at 60% scheduled hours with full pay. Lumora provides private lactation rooms in all offices.

## 4. Medical Leave and Disability

Employees who cannot work for more than 7 consecutive calendar days because of their own serious health condition should contact the Leave team to start a medical leave. Short-term disability (STD) pays 60% of base salary after a 7-day waiting period, for up to 12 weeks (see Benefits Overview, POL-008, Section 7.1). Employees may use sick leave or PTO during the waiting period and to supplement STD pay up to 100%.

In the United States, eligible employees may also be entitled to job-protected leave under the Family and Medical Leave Act (FMLA) and state leave laws. The Leave team determines eligibility and sends the required notices within 5 business days of the request.

## 5. Family Care Leave

Employees with at least 6 months of service may take up to **4 weeks of paid family care leave** per calendar year to care for a family member with a serious health condition. Additional unpaid leave may be available under FMLA or local law.

## 6. Bereavement Leave

| Relationship | Paid bereavement leave |
|---|---|
| Spouse or partner, child, parent, sibling, or anyone living in the employee's household | 5 workdays |
| Grandparent, grandchild, in-law, aunt, uncle, niece, nephew | 3 workdays |
| Pregnancy loss (employee or partner) | 5 workdays |

Bereavement leave need not be taken consecutively but must be used within 3 months of the loss. Employees should tell their manager and record the leave in PeopleHub. No documentation is required.

## 7. Jury Duty and Witness Leave

Employees summoned for jury duty or subpoenaed as a witness receive their regular pay for up to **10 workdays** per year. Employees must provide a copy of the summons to their manager and record the leave in PeopleHub. Any jury fees received may be kept by the employee.

## 8. Voting and Civic Leave

US employees may take up to **2 hours of paid time** to vote in a public election if their schedule does not allow them to vote outside working hours. Employees who volunteer as election poll workers may take up to 1 paid day per election.

## 9. Military Leave

Employees who serve in the uniformed services receive job-protected leave in accordance with USERRA (US) or applicable reservist legislation (Canada, UK). Lumora pays the difference between military pay and the employee's base salary for up to 30 days per year.

## 10. Personal Unpaid Leave

Employees with at least 1 year of service may request up to **8 weeks** of unpaid personal leave once every 3 years for reasons such as travel, education, or personal matters. Requests require manager and department director approval and should be submitted at least 60 days in advance. Benefits continue during unpaid personal leave, but the employee must pay their share of premiums. PTO does not accrue during unpaid leave.

## 11. How to Request Leave

1. For planned leave (parental, personal), discuss timing with your manager.
2. Submit a leave request in PeopleHub under **Time Off → Leave of Absence**. This opens a case with the Leave team.
3. Upload any required documents. Medical information goes only to the Leave team.
4. The Leave team confirms the leave type, pay, and dates in writing within 5 business days.

## 12. Protection From Retaliation

Lumora prohibits retaliation against any employee for requesting or taking leave. Concerns about retaliation should be raised under the Workplace Conduct Policy (POL-012) or directly with People Operations.
