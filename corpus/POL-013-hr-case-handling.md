# HR Case Handling and Escalation Procedure

> Lumora Systems, Inc. is a fictional company. This document is synthetic and was written for an AI engineering course project.

## 1. Purpose

This procedure explains how employees raise questions and requests with People Operations, how HR tickets are categorized and prioritized, what response times to expect, and when a matter is escalated. It also sets rules for automated assistants, such as the Lumora HR Assistant, that help employees find answers and open tickets.

## 2. Self-Service First

Many questions can be answered from the policy library and PeopleHub. Employees should check:

- PeopleHub for their own PTO balance, benefits elections, pay statements, and work arrangement;
- the policy library for rules and eligibility;
- the HR Assistant, which answers policy questions with citations to the policy library and can look up the employee's own records.

When self-service does not resolve the question, employees should open an HR ticket.

## 3. Ticket Categories

| Category | Typical topics | Handled by |
|---|---|---|
| Time Off | PTO balance errors, short-notice requests, blackout disputes | People Operations |
| Benefits | Enrollment, life events, coverage questions | Total Rewards |
| Leave | Parental, medical, family care, personal leave | Leave team |
| Work Location | Temporary work in another state, relocation | People Operations + Payroll |
| Global Mobility | Work from abroad reviews, international assignments | Global Mobility |
| Payroll | Pay errors, tax withholding | Payroll |
| Expenses | Claim disputes, stipend eligibility | Finance |
| Employment Records | Classification, title, manager changes | People Operations |
| Accommodations | Workplace accommodations | Accommodations team |
| Employee Relations | Harassment, discrimination, retaliation, bullying, conflicts | Employee Relations (restricted) |

## 4. Priority and Response Times

| Priority | Examples | First response | Target resolution |
|---|---|---|---|
| **P1 – Urgent** | Safety concerns, harassment or discrimination reports, retaliation, threats | 1 business day | Investigation starts within 5 business days |
| **P2 – High** | Pay errors, missing benefits coverage, leave starting within 10 days, Global Mobility requests with travel within 30 days | 2 business days | 5 business days |
| **P3 – Standard** | Policy clarifications, balance corrections, eligibility questions | 3 business days | 10 business days |
| **P4 – Low** | Information requests, document copies | 5 business days | 15 business days |

Any ticket in the Employee Relations category is automatically P1.

## 5. When to Escalate

A request must be escalated to a human People Operations partner, rather than answered from policy alone, when:

1. it involves possible harassment, discrimination, bullying, retaliation, violence, or a safety risk;
2. the employee describes a medical condition, disability, or accommodation need;
3. the answer depends on a judgment about a specific person's conduct or performance;
4. the policy is silent or ambiguous for the employee's situation, or two policies appear to conflict;
5. the employee disputes a decision already made by their manager or People Operations;
6. the request involves pay corrections, legal matters, immigration, or tax advice;
7. the employee asks for an exception to a policy.

If anyone is in immediate danger, the employee should contact local emergency services first.

## 6. Rules for Automated Assistants

The Lumora HR Assistant and any other automated tool used by People Operations must:

- answer policy questions **only** from the official policy library and cite the document ID and section for each policy fact;
- clearly separate **policy facts** from **recommendations or next steps**;
- say when the policy library does not cover a question, rather than guessing, and suggest opening a ticket;
- show employees only their **own** records;
- never submit, approve, or change a PeopleHub request, and never create a ticket or send a message on the employee's behalf **without the employee's explicit confirmation**;
- never investigate, make findings about, or give opinions on the merits of an Employee Relations matter; instead, explain the reporting channels and offer to open a confidential Employee Relations case;
- never give personal tax, legal, immigration, or medical advice.

## 7. What a Good Ticket Includes

To help People Operations resolve a ticket quickly, include:

- your employee ID;
- the category;
- a short summary of the request and the relevant dates;
- the policy sections you have already reviewed;
- any documents (upload them in the ticket, not by email).

Do not include medical details in a ticket unless it is a Leave or Accommodations ticket, which are restricted to the relevant team.

## 8. Escalation Path for Delays

If a ticket misses its first-response time, the employee may reply to the ticket requesting escalation. It is then routed to the team lead. If a P1 or P2 ticket is still unanswered after a further business day, it is routed to the Head of People Operations.

## 9. Closing Tickets

Tickets are closed when the employee confirms the issue is resolved, or automatically 5 business days after People Operations provides a resolution with no reply. Employees can reopen a ticket within 30 days. Employee Relations cases are closed only by the investigator.

## 10. Records and Privacy

Ticket records are retained for 7 years. Employee Relations, Leave, and Accommodations records are restricted to their teams and treated as Restricted data (POL-006, Section 3).
