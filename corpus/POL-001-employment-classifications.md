# Employment Classifications and Eligibility

> Lumora Systems, Inc. is a fictional company. This document is synthetic and was written for an AI engineering course project.

## 1. Purpose

This policy defines the employment categories used at Lumora Systems, Inc. ("Lumora" or "the Company") and explains how a worker's classification determines eligibility for paid time off, benefits, remote work, stipends, and other programs. Many other Lumora policies refer back to the definitions in this document, so when a policy says "eligible employees" it means the categories listed as eligible in the tables below unless that policy states otherwise.

Classification is assigned by People Operations at the time of hire and is recorded in PeopleHub, the Company's HR system of record. An employee's manager cannot change a classification; changes must be requested through an HR ticket and approved by People Operations.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all workers engaged by Lumora in the United States, Canada, and the United Kingdom, including workers who are fully remote. Country-specific statutory requirements take precedence where they provide a greater benefit to the worker than this policy.

## 3. Employment Categories

### 3.1 Full-Time Employee

A full-time employee is a W-2 (US), T4 (Canada), or PAYE (UK) employee who is regularly scheduled to work 40 hours per week, recorded as an FTE (full-time equivalent) of 1.0. Full-time employees are eligible for all programs described in Lumora policies, subject to any tenure requirements stated in a specific policy.

### 3.2 Part-Time Employee

A part-time employee is a W-2, T4, or PAYE employee regularly scheduled to work fewer than 40 hours per week. Part-time employees have an FTE between 0.5 and 0.9. Their eligibility depends on FTE:

- Part-time employees with an FTE of **0.75 or higher** (30 or more hours per week) are "benefits-eligible part-time" employees. They are eligible for medical, dental, and vision coverage and for the 401(k) plan.
- Part-time employees with an FTE **below 0.75** are eligible for the 401(k) plan, sick leave, and the Employee Assistance Program, but not for medical, dental, or vision coverage.
- All part-time employees accrue paid time off on a prorated basis (PTO accrual multiplied by FTE).
- Part-time employees are **not** eligible for the one-time home office stipend or the annual learning budget unless their FTE is 0.75 or higher.

### 3.3 Intern

An intern is a temporary employee engaged for a defined program, typically 10 to 16 weeks. Interns are paid hourly and are W-2, T4, or PAYE employees. Interns:

- do **not** accrue PTO, but receive sick leave (5 days per program) and paid company holidays that fall within their program dates;
- are **not** eligible for medical, dental, vision, the 401(k) match, stipends, tuition reimbursement, or the learning budget;
- are **not** eligible for remote work arrangements outside their assigned office, except with written approval from the University Programs lead;
- may use the Employee Assistance Program.

### 3.4 Contractor

A contractor is an independent contractor or a worker supplied by an agency. Contractors are not employees of Lumora. Their working arrangements, time off, equipment, and expenses are governed entirely by their contract or statement of work. Contractors:

- are **not** eligible for PTO, company benefits, stipends, the learning budget, or the work-from-abroad program;
- may be reimbursed for expenses only where the statement of work explicitly allows it;
- must follow the Information Security Policy (POL-006) and the Workplace Conduct Policy (POL-012) while engaged;
- may use the Employee Assistance Program while actively engaged.

Questions about contractor arrangements must be directed to the Vendor Management team, not People Operations.

## 5. Introductory Period

All new employees (full-time, part-time, and interns) complete a 90-day introductory period that begins on their start date. During the introductory period:

- employees accrue PTO from their first day but may not use more than 3 days of PTO before day 90 without manager and HR approval;
- employees are not eligible to request remote work from a location other than their assigned office or home address (see POL-004, Section 4);
- employees are not eligible for the work-from-abroad program (see POL-005);
- managers complete 30-, 60-, and 90-day check-ins as described in the Onboarding Guide (POL-010).

## 6. Tenure

Tenure is measured from an employee's most recent hire date as recorded in PeopleHub. Tenure determines PTO accrual tier (POL-002), eligibility for tuition reimbursement and parental leave, and certain approval thresholds. Rehired employees whose break in service was less than 12 months have their prior service restored for accrual purposes.

## 7. Work Location and Office Assignment

Every employee has a **work location** recorded in PeopleHub. The work location is either one of Lumora's offices or a registered home address for remote employees. Lumora's offices are:

- **Atlanta, Georgia, USA** (headquarters)
- **Austin, Texas, USA**
- **Toronto, Ontario, Canada**
- **London, United Kingdom**

The work location determines the employee's payroll jurisdiction, the holiday calendar that applies (POL-003), and the tax and legal obligations that the Company must meet. Working from any location other than the registered work location for more than the thresholds in POL-004 and POL-005 requires approval, because it can create tax, payroll, immigration, and legal obligations for both the employee and the Company.

## 8. Exempt and Non-Exempt Status (US)

US employees are classified as exempt or non-exempt under the Fair Labor Standards Act. Non-exempt employees must record all hours worked in PeopleHub and are paid overtime for hours over 40 in a workweek. Non-exempt employees may not work outside their scheduled hours, including checking email, without prior manager approval. Exempt employees are not eligible for overtime.

## 9. Changes in Classification

Changes in classification (for example, full-time to part-time, or intern to full-time) take effect on the first day of the pay period after approval. Changes may affect PTO accrual rate, benefits eligibility, and stipend eligibility. When a change causes loss of benefits eligibility, coverage ends on the last day of the month in which the change occurs, and the employee will receive information about continuation coverage.

## 10. Questions

Questions about classification should be submitted through an HR ticket in the "Employment Records" category. Employees should not rely on informal guidance from colleagues about their eligibility; PeopleHub and People Operations are the authoritative sources.
