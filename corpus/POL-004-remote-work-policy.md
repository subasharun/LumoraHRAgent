# Remote and Hybrid Work Policy

> Lumora Systems, Inc. is a fictional company. This document is synthetic and was written for an AI engineering course project.

## 1. Purpose

Lumora supports flexible work where the role allows it. This policy describes the work arrangements available to employees, who is eligible, how temporary work from a different location within the employee's home country is handled, and the responsibilities that come with working outside a Lumora office. Working from a **different country** is covered by the Work From Abroad and Cross-Border Work Policy (POL-005). Security requirements for working remotely are in the Information Security Policy (POL-006).

## 2. Work Arrangements

Each employee has one of three work arrangements recorded in PeopleHub:

- **Office-based:** The employee works from an assigned Lumora office at least 4 days per week.
- **Hybrid:** The employee works from an assigned Lumora office on **2 anchor days per week** set by their team (typically Tuesday and Wednesday) and may work from their registered home address on other days.
- **Remote:** The employee works from their registered home address and is not expected to come into an office regularly. Remote employees may be asked to travel to an office or team gathering up to 4 times per year, with travel expenses reimbursed under POL-007.

A work arrangement is agreed between the employee and their manager, approved by the department director, and recorded in PeopleHub. Changing a work arrangement requires a new approval.

## 3. Eligibility

### 3.1 Role eligibility

Each role at Lumora is designated as either **remote-eligible** or **office-required**. Office-required roles include facilities, hardware lab, and certain IT support positions. Employees in office-required roles may work remotely only on an occasional basis (no more than 2 days per month) with manager approval.

### 3.2 Employee eligibility

To request a hybrid or remote arrangement, or to request temporary work from another location, an employee must:

1. be a full-time or part-time employee (interns and contractors are not eligible; see POL-001);
2. have completed the 90-day introductory period;
3. not be on a formal performance improvement plan; and
4. hold a remote-eligible role.

## 4. Temporary Work From Another Location in Your Home Country

Employees sometimes want to work temporarily from somewhere other than their registered work location, such as a family home in another state or province. Because working in another state or province can create payroll tax, withholding, and employment law obligations, Lumora applies the following thresholds. Thresholds are counted in **calendar days per calendar year**, summed across all temporary locations outside the registered work location's state or province.

| Total days per calendar year in another state/province | Approval required |
|---|---|
| Up to 14 calendar days | Manager notification in PeopleHub (no approval needed) |
| 15 to 30 calendar days | Manager approval |
| 31 to 60 calendar days | Manager approval **and** People Operations + Payroll approval |
| More than 60 calendar days | Not permitted as temporary work. The employee must request a permanent change of work location. |

Requests that require People Operations and Payroll approval must be submitted at least **30 calendar days** before the first day of temporary work, because Payroll may need to register in the new state and set up tax withholding. People Operations will respond within 10 business days.

### 4.1 Permanent relocation within the home country

An employee who wants to move permanently to a new state or province must submit a **Work Location Change** HR ticket at least 45 calendar days before the move. Lumora does not employ people in every state. The current list of states and provinces where Lumora can employ people is maintained by People Operations. Relocation may change the employee's pay zone, holiday calendar, and benefits options.

## 5. Responsibilities When Working Remotely

Employees working remotely, on a hybrid basis, or from a temporary location must:

- be reachable during their team's core collaboration hours (10:00 a.m. to 3:00 p.m. in the team's primary time zone);
- maintain a safe, quiet workspace suitable for confidential conversations;
- use only Lumora-issued devices for Company work and connect through the Company VPN when on any network other than a Lumora office network (see POL-006, Section 5);
- never work from public locations on Restricted data (see POL-006, Section 3);
- keep their PeopleHub address and emergency contact up to date;
- report any work-related injury at home to People Operations within 24 hours.

## 6. Home Office Setup

Remote and hybrid employees who meet the eligibility requirements in the Expense Reimbursement Policy (POL-007, Section 6) may claim a one-time home office stipend. Standard equipment such as laptops and monitors is issued by IT (see Equipment and IT Policy, POL-011) and is not purchased by the employee.

## 7. Time Zones and Scheduling

Employees working temporarily in a different time zone must still meet core collaboration hours in their team's primary time zone unless their manager agrees to a different schedule in writing. Non-exempt employees must not work outside approved hours (see POL-001, Section 8).

## 8. Reviewing and Ending Arrangements

Managers may review work arrangements at any time and may end a hybrid or remote arrangement with at least 30 calendar days' written notice, or immediately where there is a security incident, a significant performance concern, or a change in role. Ending an arrangement must not be retaliatory or discriminatory.

## 9. Coworking Spaces

Remote employees may use a coworking space up to the monthly limit in POL-007, Section 6.3, provided the space has a private room available for confidential calls. Coworking spaces count as public networks for security purposes, so the VPN is always required.

## 10. How to Request

1. Discuss the request with your manager.
2. Submit the request in PeopleHub under **Work Arrangements → Temporary Location** (for Section 4) or **Work Arrangements → Change Arrangement** (for Section 2).
3. If the request requires People Operations and Payroll approval, PeopleHub automatically opens an HR ticket in the "Work Location" category.
4. Do not begin working from the new location until all required approvals are shown as complete in PeopleHub.

## 11. Frequently Asked Questions

**Can I work from my parents' house in another state for two weeks?** Yes, if you are eligible under Section 3. Up to 14 calendar days per year only requires notifying your manager in PeopleHub.

**Can I work from another state for six weeks?** Six weeks (42 calendar days) falls in the 31-to-60-day tier, so it requires manager approval and People Operations + Payroll approval, submitted at least 30 calendar days in advance.

**Does this policy cover working from another country?** No. Any work from outside your registered work location's country is governed by POL-005.
